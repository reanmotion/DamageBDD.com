*Step-based execution model

We calculate pricing based on the number of steps and their implementations.  
Each step is priced individually, depending on the resources used and its complexity.

Running one step 10 times will deduct 10 Damage Tokens.  
If you run steps concurrently, each execution still counts toward your token balance.
